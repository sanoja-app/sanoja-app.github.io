How to say "sorry" and "excuse me" in Finnish

anteeksi
Say it: AHN-tayk-see. In Finnish, stress is always on the first syllable.

Anteeksi covers both "sorry" and "excuse me". Casually, people say sori.

Sorry, excuse me, pardon

FinnishEnglishWhen
anteeksisorry / excuse meEveryday, polite
sorisorryCasual, with friends
pahoittelenI apologiseFormal, written apologies
anteeksi, mitä?pardon?When you did not catch something
ei se mitäänit's nothing / no problemReplying to an apology

Getting someone's attention

Anteeksi is also how you stop someone to ask a question, the way English uses "excuse me": Anteeksi, missä on asema? ("Excuse me, where is the station?"). It comes from the phrase antaa anteeksi, "to forgive".

Example sentences

  • Anteeksi, missä on asema?Excuse me, where is the station?
  • Anteeksi, että olen myöhässä.Sorry I'm late.
  • Anteeksi, mitä sanoit?Sorry, what did you say?
  • Sori!Sorry!

Common questions

How do you say "sorry" in Finnish?

Anteeksi. Among friends, sori is common.

How do you say "excuse me" in Finnish?

Also anteeksi. The same word works for apologising and for getting someone's attention.
