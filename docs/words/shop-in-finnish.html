"Shop" in Finnish: kauppa, and the three forms you need

kauppa
Say it: KOW-ppa. In Finnish, stress is always on the first syllable.

Kauppa means "shop" or "store" (and also "trade"). The place forms are kaupassa (in the shop), kauppaan (to the shop) and kaupasta (from the shop).

In, to and from

Finnish uses endings where English uses prepositions. This is the pattern you will meet again and again with places:

FormMeaningExample
kaupassain / at the shopOlen kaupassa. I'm at the shop.
kauppaanto the shopMenen kauppaan. I'm going to the shop.
kaupastafrom the shopTulen kaupasta. I'm coming from the shop.

Notice that the double pp becomes a single p in kaupassa and kaupasta. This is called consonant gradation, and you will see it in many Finnish words.

Common questions

How do you say "shop" or "store" in Finnish?

Kauppa. A grocery store is a ruokakauppa.

How do you say "in the shop" in Finnish?

Kaupassa. "To the shop" is kauppaan and "from the shop" is kaupasta.
