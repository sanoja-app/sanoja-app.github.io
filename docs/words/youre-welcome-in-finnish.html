How to say "you're welcome" and "please" in Finnish

ole hyvä
Say it: OH-leh HÜ-vä. In Finnish, stress is always on the first syllable.

Ole hyvä means "you're welcome". It also means "here you are" when handing something over, and "please go ahead".

One phrase, three jobs

SituationFinnishEnglish
Someone thanks youOle hyvä.You're welcome.
You hand something overOle hyvä.Here you are.
You invite someone to go ahead or help themselvesOle hyvä.Please, go ahead.

Informal and formal

Ole hyvä is the singular, informal form (literally "be good"). To several people, or politely to one, use olkaa hyvä. You will see this at buffets and on signs, for example Olkaa hyvä ja istukaa ("Please sit down").

Other ways to reply to thanks

In casual speech you can answer kiitos with ei kestä or ei se mitään ("it's nothing"). Ole hyvä is the safe default.

Example sentences

  • Ole hyvä.Here you are. / You're welcome.
  • Olkaa hyvä ja istukaa.Please sit down.
  • Ole hyvä, ota kahvia.Please, have some coffee.
  • Kiitos! — Ole hyvä.Thanks! — You're welcome.
