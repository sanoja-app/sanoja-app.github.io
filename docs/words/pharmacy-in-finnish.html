"Pharmacy" in Finnish: apteekki

apteekki
Say it: AP-teek-ki. In Finnish, stress is always on the first syllable.

Apteekki means "pharmacy". Medicine is lääke, and a prescription is a resepti.

Asking for something

Use tarvitsen ("I need") followed by the item in the partitive: Tarvitsen särkylääkettä ("I need a painkiller"). You can also ask Onko teillä...? ("Do you have ...?").

Example sentences

  • Missä on lähin apteekki?Where is the nearest pharmacy?
  • Tarvitsen särkylääkettä.I need a painkiller.
  • Minulla on resepti.I have a prescription.
  • Onko apteekki auki?Is the pharmacy open?
