Colors in Finnish

punainen
Say it: POO-nai-nen. In Finnish, stress is always on the first syllable.

Red is punainen, blue sininen, green vihreä, yellow keltainen, black musta, white valkoinen.

Colors agree with the noun

Finnish adjectives take the same number and case as the noun they describe. So you get punainen talo ("a red house"), punaisessa talossa ("in the red house") and punaiset talot ("red houses"). The dictionary form ends in -nen, but that ending changes to -s- in most other forms.

Vaaleanpunainen ("pink") is literally "light red", from vaalea ("light") plus punainen.

Example sentences

  • Talo on punainen.The house is red.
  • Suomen lippu on sininen ja valkoinen.The Finnish flag is blue and white.
  • Onko sinulla sininen takki?Do you have a blue coat?
  • Haluan mustaa kahvia.I'd like black coffee.

What colors is the Finnish flag?

Sininen ja valkoinen (blue and white).
