"Water" in Finnish: vesi, and why it changes shape

vesi
Say it: VEH-si. In Finnish, stress is always on the first syllable.

Vesi means "water". Its other forms use a different stem: veden, vettä, vedessä.

The forms of vesi

CaseFormUsed for
Basicvesiwater (as a subject)
Genitivevedenof the water
Partitivevettäsome water, for example juon vettä
Invedessäin the water
Intoveteeninto the water
Fromvedestäfrom the water

Notice that the stem changes from vesi to vete- / vede-. This kind of change is common in Finnish and is a big reason why looking up forms you meet while reading is so useful.

Water words

Hanavesi is tap water, kylmä vesi is cold water, and lasillinen vettä is "a glass of water". A lake is a järvi and the sea is meri.

Example sentences

  • Haluaisin lasillisen vettä.I would like a glass of water.
  • Juon vettä.I'm drinking water.
  • Vesi on kylmää.The water is cold.
  • Hanavesi on hyvää.The tap water is good.

Common questions

How do you say "water" in Finnish?

Vesi.

How do you say "a glass of water, please" in Finnish?

Lasillinen vettä, kiitos.
